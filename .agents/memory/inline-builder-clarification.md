name: Inline builder questions
description: Clarification requests and customer answers belong in the builder chat.
---

Builder clarification questions and customer replies must stay in the chat transcript. Do not direct customers to a separate question panel.

**Why:** The user corrected copy that pointed to a “question panel above”; customers expect to answer in the chat where their build request lives.

**How to apply:** Keep pre-build questions, follow-up answers, and assistant instructions visible and actionable in the builder chat.
